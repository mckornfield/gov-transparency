A rough estimate for the whole United States, fiscal year 2024. It counts only programs whose dollars could be tied to an age: Social Security, Medicare, Medicaid (the federal share), veterans' benefits and health care, and education. Most of the rest of the budget (defense, interest on the debt, food aid, housing, tax credits) is not allocated to an age here, so these are floors, not the whole picture. It shows who received money, not who paid taxes, and it cannot show whether anyone was better or worse off. The brackets follow the health data (the most limiting source); the generation labels are only a guide to who is in each bracket.
