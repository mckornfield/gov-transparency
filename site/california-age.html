Who receives California state dollars, by age

A rough estimate for the State of California, fiscal year 2023–24 (July 2023 to June 2024). It counts the state's own money (General Fund, special funds and bond funds) for programs whose dollars could be tied to an age: K-12 schools, colleges and universities, Medi-Cal, developmental services, in-home care, the SSI state supplement, CalWORKs and child care. Money the state sends to school districts and counties is counted here, because the state pays it. Federal money that flows through the same departments is left out: it is on the federal page, so the two pages do not count the same dollar twice. Local money, such as the property-tax share of school funding, is not here either. The totals are floors, not the whole picture, and a rough split can move dollars between brackets. The page shows who received money, not who paid taxes, and it cannot show whether anyone was better or worse off. The brackets match the federal page; the generation labels are only a guide to who is in each bracket.
