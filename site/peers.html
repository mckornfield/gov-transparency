The peers are 11 US cities of roughly 0.6 to 1.7 million people, chosen to be broadly similar and to cover several regions. A higher number is not waste: it can reflect higher prices or wages, or a different mix of services. Fiscal years end in different months and nothing is adjusted for cost of living. This compares San Diego with other cities, not with the private sector. "Administration, courts & inspection" is a broad Census category that includes spending not classified elsewhere, so it is the least comparable row: part of any gap there may reflect how cities report.
