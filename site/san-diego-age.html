A rough estimate of what the local governments serving a resident of the City of San Diego spend on people of each age: the county's school districts, the County of San Diego and the City of San Diego, each shown separately. Each government's dollars are what it spends, whoever paid for them; the tables mark how much of each program is state, federal or local money. Because of that, this page overlaps the state and federal pages (state school aid is state spending there and school district spending here), so the three pages cannot be added together. The years differ: school figures are 2023–24 actual spending, and the county and city figures are 2024–25 adopted budgets, the nearest year each publishes as data. School and county dollars are divided by county residents and city dollars by city residents, so the per-person bars describe someone living in the City of San Diego; other cities' own spending is not here. Most county and city spending (law enforcement, roads, water, administration) is not tied to an age and is listed below, not spread. The page shows who received money, not who paid taxes, and it cannot show whether anyone was better or worse off.
