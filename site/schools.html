Where school money goes

Loading…

The 42 school districts in San Diego County and the County Office of Education, fiscal year 2023–24, as each filed its unaudited year-end accounts with the state. The headline is the general fund, the day-to-day operating budget; construction, bond repayment, cafeterias and other separate funds are listed further down so they do not blur the operating picture. Benefits are what the district pays on top of salaries: pension contributions, health coverage, Social Security and insurance. Part of the teacher-pension line is paid by the state on the district's behalf and is recorded in the district's books as both income and spending. Charter schools that file their own accounts are not included, and their students are not in the student counts; the County Office of Education's spending is in the county total but it has no student count of its own, so the county-wide figure per student runs a little high. The ledger has dollars only, so it cannot show pay per employee or how many people were employed, and nothing here says whether any amount is too much or too little.
